# 8. Reliability & Operations

This section focuses on making systems survive failure and stay operable in production.

## What this section covers

- High availability
- Fault tolerance
- Disaster recovery
- Observability: logs, metrics, and traces
- Distributed tracing
- Zero-downtime deployment
- Blue-green and canary deployment

## How to study it

1. Learn how systems detect and respond to failure.
2. Understand the difference between availability and correctness.
3. Connect operations practice to real incidents and recovery plans.
